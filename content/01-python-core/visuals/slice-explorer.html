Slice explorer

Pick one of the slices above, or type your own start, stop and step. The cursor walks the slice; the lit cells are the result.

numbers[2:5]→[3, 4, 5]
Start2first index taken
Stop5never taken
Step1stride and direction
Items3in the result

Observe: the stop index is never taken, so numbers[a:b] has b - a items. A missing start or stop means "from the far end", and a negative step walks right to left.
